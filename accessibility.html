Accessibility.

Navigate at your pace

The animated introduction includes a Skip button. Links and buttons support keyboard focus. Project cards link directly to their detail pages.

Exploring the gallery

The immersive gallery supports dragging, scrolling and arrow keys. You can also browse the standard project collection and its filters.

Motion and compatibility

This concept retains substantial animation from the reference experience. Where WebGL is unavailable, the immersive gallery uses an interactive image grid. Accessibility has not been independently audited.
